Reflection

Written Evaluation

Working on this project, I learned a lot about myself as a designer and about how I develop ideas through fashion. At the beginning, I was mainly drawn to the visual contrast between ballet and metal because they felt so different and almost opposite. Ballet appeared soft, elegant, and fragile, while metal felt cold, heavy, and powerful. However, through research and experimentation, I started to realize that the concept became much more personal and emotional than I originally expected.

One of the most important parts of my development was researching ballerinas and understanding how much discipline, pain, and physical strength exist behind their graceful appearance. It changed my perspective on femininity, helping me understand that softness does not equal weakness. This idea became the core of my collection and shaped my approach to combining vulnerability with strength.

Another strong influence was visiting Hajime Sorayama's Sexy Robot exhibition in Shanghai. The futuristic metallic surfaces and the relationship between the human body and metal encouraged me to experiment more with texture and material choices. It also gave me more confidence to try unexpected combinations and push my ideas further.

I also developed my technical skills through this process. Working with different fabrics and metallic elements was challenging, especially when trying to keep a balance in the silhouettes without making them feel too heavy or uncomfortable. Some experiments worked, others didn't, but this helped me understand the value of testing and refinement. Overall, this project helped me become more confident in my creative identity. It showed me how to express emotion and meaning through fashion, not just visually, but also through concept, material, and detail.
